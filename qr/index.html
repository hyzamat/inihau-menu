WhatsApp ordering

  • Customers tap + on dishes, then Send order on WhatsApp — the order arrives at +966 53 562 7615 with items, total, pickup/delivery, name and address.
  • Link for social media posts (opens the order screen directly):
    inihaumenu.hizarc.com/#order
  • Link to a single dish, e.g. inihaumenu.hizarc.com/#bulalo

Printing tips

  • Print on A5 paper at 100% / Actual size (not "Fit to page").
  • Glossy or matte photo paper looks best; laminate it for tables.
  • Keep the QR code at least 3 cm wide if you make it smaller.
  • Test with your phone camera after printing.
  • Visiting card: ask the print shop for 90 × 50 mm, double-sided, from the PDF (it already has 3 mm bleed).
